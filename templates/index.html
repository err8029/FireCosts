Disclaimer

Burnt area is a rough proxy built by buffering active-fire detections, not an official burn-perimeter product. Forest/vegetation burnt is estimated from OpenStreetMap land-cover tagging (forest, scrub/heath, grassland, farmland) clipped to that same proxy — coverage and categorization in OSM varies by area, so this is an estimate, not a substitute for a real land-cover product. The report's vegetation-value table prices each category's burnt area at the price/hectare set in Prices — rough illustrative figures, not a market appraisal or timber valuation. Building counts are estimates, not a substitute for official damage assessments. Value-lost estimates use real building areas from Spain's public Catastro service crossed with an assumed price per m² (set in Prices) — Catastro does not expose official assessed values to the public, and this is not a substitute for a real appraisal. Only works for locations in Spain; buildings without a cadastral match fall back to their OSM footprint area (× floor count, when OSM has it) at the fallback price.
