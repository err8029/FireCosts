Burnt area is a rough proxy built by buffering active-fire detections, not an official burn-perimeter product. Building counts are estimates, not a substitute for official damage assessments. Value-lost estimates use real building areas from Spain's public Catastro service crossed with an assumed price per m² (editable above) — Catastro does not expose official assessed values to the public, and this is not a substitute for a real appraisal. Only works for locations in Spain; buildings without a cadastral match fall back to their OSM footprint area (× floor count, when OSM has it) at the fallback price.
